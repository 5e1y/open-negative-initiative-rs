scanner_nikon_d850.json: the Nikon D850's measured spectral sensitivities, from the rawtoaces-data project of the
Academy Software Foundation (https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/rawtoaces-data, commit 28801d1,
data/camera/Nikon_D850_380_780_5.json), rounded to six decimals and laid red, green, blue; its colour matrix is
LibRaw's for that camera.
