Changes to the spektrafilm data shipped in this folder
======================================================

Source: spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato, https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm,
commit 3bb2c2d (2026-06-07). Licence: SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt (CC BY-SA 4.0), kept unchanged.

- irradiance_xy_tc.npy: shipped unchanged (Jakob and Hanika's spectra, see SPECTRA_LICENCE.txt).
- kodak_portra_160.json, kodak_portra_400.json, kodak_ektar_100.json, kodak_gold_200.json,
  kodak_ultramax_400.json, kodak_vision3_50d.json, kodak_vision3_500t.json, fujifilm_c200.json,

2026-11-22, by the Open Negative Initiative contributors:

- kodak_portra_800_push1.json, kodak_portra_800_push2.json: shipped unchanged, for the virtual films of Portra 800
  pushed one and two stops (their dyes are Portra 800's; their curves are their own).
- fujifilm_provia_100f.json, fujifilm_velvia_100.json, kodak_ektachrome_100.json, kodak_kodachrome_64.json: shipped
  unchanged, the colour reversal films spektrafilm profiles, for the catalogue's Provia 100, Velvia 100, Ektachrome and
  Kodachrome 64. No part of Open Negative Initiative reads them yet: a slide's pipeline has no per-stock stage.
- Every colour negative film profile at commit 3bb2c2d is now shipped. Not shipped: the prints, spektrafilm's papers
  kodak_ektacolor_edge.json and its cine print films kodak_2383.json and kodak_2393.json.

Derivatives Open Negative Initiative computes from them at run time, never written to a file
(crates/oni-pipeline/src/film/bake.rs):

- Each stock's reference light, its profile's reference_illuminant: D55, or for "T" (Vision3 200T and 500T) NIST's
  incandescent lamp as colour-science 0.4.6 ships it (CQS 7.4), the spectrum spektrafilm reads for "T", scaled to a
  mean of 1 (crates/oni-pipeline/src/film/cie.rs).
- The light per chromaticity: the spectra contracted with the profile's sensitivities through its
  band-pass window (its window parameters, each channel's response to its reference light kept), the input gamut
  compression (radial, Reinhard knee of power 6 toward the spectral locus) laid in, 192 x 192 nodes
  in 32-bit floats, read bilinearly.
- The density curves above their least density, and the curves before the DIR couplers act
  (spektrafilm's colour-negative coupler matrix), so a developed grey lands on the profile's curves.
- The scan: the dyes' and the base's densities on 405 to 695 nm, where the profile measures both,
  read under D50 by the CIE 1931 observer, adapted to D65 by Bradford, in linear Rec. 2020.
- The dye separation's dyes (crates/oni-pipeline/src/film/dyes.rs): each profile's dye and base densities on
  the wavelengths it measures both, read by the 1931 observer under D50 (the virtual negative's scan) and by
  that scan's Rec. 2020 matrix; the matrix of each stock's dye amounts at a unit of every dye; and a generic
  colour-negative set, the mean of the nine C-41 profiles above (Portra 160, 400 and 800, Ektar 100, Gold 200,
  UltraMax 400, C200, Pro 400H, Superia X-TRA 400) at each wavelength more than half of them measure.
- The paper (crates/oni-pipeline/src/paper.rs): the enlarger's lamp, spektrafilm's TH-KG3 (a 3400 K blackbody
  through the KG3 curve read by scipy's Akima interpolation, its mean 1), its dichroic filters (spektrafilm's
  custom erf edges), each paper's layer sensitivities times that lamp on the wavelengths a stock measures its dyes
  and base, its density curves from its fitted model (sums of normal integrals) on its 256 nodes, and its dyes and
  base on 405 to 695 nm read under D50 by the 1931 observer, adapted to D65 by CAT02, in linear Rec. 2020.
