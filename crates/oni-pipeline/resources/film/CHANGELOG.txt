What Open Negative Initiative keeps from spektrafilm
===================================================

Source: spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato, https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm,
commit 3bb2c2d (2026-06-07).

No spektrafilm film or paper profile is shipped: every film stock and paper ONI prints is written from its maker's
data sheet (datasheet/, SOURCES.txt there). What remains:

- irradiance_xy_tc.npy: Jakob and Hanika's spectra as the spektrafilm project ships them, unchanged (licence:
  SPECTRA_LICENCE.txt).
- schott_kg3.csv: spektrafilm's data/filters/heat_absorbing/schott/KG3.csv (Schott KG3's transmittance as Newport
  publishes it), unchanged, for the enlarger's lamp; outside spektrafilm's profiles, under its code's licence, the
  GPL-3.0.
- spektrafilm's model, which ONI's bake follows in its own code (crates/oni-pipeline/src/film/bake.rs and
  crates/oni-pipeline/src/paper.rs): the CAT16 adaptation the film is exposed through, the input gamut compression,
  the colour negative's DIR coupler matrix, the TH-KG3 enlarger lamp and the custom dichroic filters, and the CAT02
  adaptation the print is viewed through.
- The references formats 54 to 59 derived from spektrafilm's sixteen colour negative profiles, kept so a digital
  photo stored then moves onto its stock's data-sheet reference with every hand's move
  (crates/oni-settings/src/migrate/datasheet.rs, and Portra 800's of format 37 in migrate/digital_film.rs): numbers
  derived from the profiles, under CC BY-SA 4.0 (SPEKTRAFILM_LICENSE.txt, unchanged).

2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22: spektrafilm's sixteen colour negative profiles, Kodachrome 64's and its five papers
(Portra Endura, Supra Endura, Ultra Endura, Endura Premier, Crystal Archive Type II) were shipped unchanged.
2026-11-22: all of them removed, each stock and paper replaced by its data-sheet profile.
