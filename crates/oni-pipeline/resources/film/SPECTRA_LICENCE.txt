irradiance_xy_tc.npy: the spectra per xy chromaticity of Wenzel Jakob and Johannes Hanika's spectral
upsampling, as vkdt's tools generate them (https://github.com/hanatos/vkdt), shipped unchanged as the
spektrafilm project ships it (https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm, commit 3bb2c2d).

irradiance_xy_otsu2018.npy: the spectra per xy chromaticity a colour negative's virtual film reads, each a reflectance
Otsu, Yamamoto and Hachisuka's 2018 basis rebuilds (colour-science 0.4.x, XYZ_to_sd_Otsu2018), lit by D55, laid on the
same chromaticity square; made by scripts/otsu-spectra.py. colour-science is BSD-3-Clause, copyright the Colour
Developers; its Otsu 2018 basis and clusters come from the paper's authors under the same terms.
