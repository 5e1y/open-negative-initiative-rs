Film and paper profiles written from the makers' data sheets
============================================================

Each .json here is written by scripts/datasheet-profiles.py from the curves traced off one official data sheet
(Kodak, Kodak Alaris, Fujifilm), as the emulsion dataset of the Open Negative Initiative contributors records them.
Each file names its sheet in "provenance": the sheet's reference, its official URL, the SHA-256 of the PDF it was
traced from, the charts read, what is measured and what is inferred. No PDF and no image of a sheet is shipped, and
no number here comes from spektrafilm.

What the files hold:

- Measured, as printed on the sheet: each layer's spectral sensitivity, the characteristic curves (Status M for a
  film, Status A for a paper, each less its least density), a film's D-min and mid-scale neutral spectral densities,
  a Vision3 sheet's and a paper's cyan, magenta and yellow dye shapes. A paper is taken only when its sheet, or an
  older edition of it, prints its characteristic curves; a display material (Fujiclear) is written as a paper, its
  densities read as its transmittance under the viewing light.
- Inferred by the script, each listed in its file: a sensitivity or a dye past the sheet's last point; a film's grey
  exposure (its ISO 5800 speed point plus the offset Kodak Gold 200's printed Log H Ref holds over its own); a
  photographic film's cyan, magenta and yellow split of its printed neutral (the Vision3 sheets' mean dyes moved and
  widened to fit it); each dye's unit (Status M or Status A approximated by normal bands); a paper's base spectrum
  (its own dyes at its curves' least densities); a paper's curve under its first printed point, where its sheet
  hides it under the others (their mean). The fourth, cyan-sensitive layer some Fujifilm sheets print is left
  out.

Licence. The files are measurements: the numbers the makers publish about their products, read off their public
technical sheets, with each sheet credited and linked. Such facts are not a work their makers' copyright covers, and
no sheet is reproduced, only values read from its charts; the files ship under the repository's licence (GPL-3.0),
with the attribution above kept. The makers' names and product names are their trademarks, used only to name the
product a profile describes. Every film stock and paper ONI prints is one of these files; nothing here derives from a
spektrafilm profile, and none is shipped.
